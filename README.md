# High Standards, Fearless Teams

An interactive leadership story for CCGL9079. You lead a fictional team at Clover, make two decisions, see the consequences, and compare them with four real cases: Wells Fargo, Netflix, Ford and Microsoft.

**Research question:** How can leaders maintain high standards without making team members afraid to fail?

1. When do high expectations motivate, and when do they discourage?
2. How does a leader's response to mistakes shape team behaviour?

## Pages

- `index.html`: the interactive story (about 4–5 minutes).
- `research.html`: the full written research, case analyses, limitations and references.

## Files

- `content.js`: story scenes, the four case analyses, Edmondson's four zones, notebook text and sources.
- `game.js`: screens, decisions, progress bar, leadership map and navigation.
- `style.css`: layout and animation.
- `world.js` / `office-canvas.js`: the original 3D office (Three.js) and its 2D fallback.
- `vendor/`: bundled Three.js (MIT licence).

## Run locally

JavaScript modules need an HTTP server: `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open http://localhost:8000.

## Notes

All Clover characters, dialogue and outcomes are fictional. Real-company examples are labelled and sourced. No names or choices are stored or sent anywhere. Keep the course submission anonymous; add UIDs only to the separate submission form.
